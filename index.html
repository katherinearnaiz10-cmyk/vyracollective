ABOUT VYRA

Victory + Rise

VYRA Collective is a creative and administrative virtual support agency built around a simple idea: people from different backgrounds rising together, using their God-given gifts, and creating victories for themselves and the businesses they serve.

Our team is made up of talented professionals from different countries, working together across borders with one shared purpose. Though we come from different places, we bring our skills, perspectives, and strengths together as one collective — supporting our clients, growing together, and creating meaningful results.

Collective reflects the people behind the work — creatives, editors, designers, virtual assistants, marketers, and support professionals working together with purpose.

God at the center. People at the heart. Excellence in the work. Victory together.

Katherine E. Arnaiz

Founder & Creative Director, VYRA Collective

Katherine E. Arnaiz is the Founder and Creative Director of VYRA Collective, bringing more than nine years of experience in customer service, business support, virtual assistance, and creative digital work.

Her professional journey began in the BPO industry, where she spent nine years working onsite and developed a strong foundation in customer service, communication, problem-solving, professionalism, and working under pressure. Those years taught her something that would later become essential to building her own company: excellent service is not simply about completing a task—it is about understanding people, taking responsibility, and delivering work with care.

Eventually, Katherine transitioned from the traditional corporate environment into the work-from-home and Virtual Assistant industry. That transition opened the door for her to work with clients and teams across different countries, including Australia, the United States, Singapore, and Korea.

Her experience expanded far beyond customer service. Over time, she worked across administrative support, social media management, content creation, graphic design, video editing, client communication, project coordination, and digital operations. Working with different businesses, cultures, industries, and expectations gave her a deeper understanding of what business owners truly need from a remote team: people who are dependable, adaptable, proactive, and genuinely invested in the work.

But VYRA Collective was not built simply because Katherine wanted to start an agency. It was built from everything she learned along the way.

After years of being the person behind the screen supporting other businesses, she began envisioning something bigger: a company where talented people could grow together, where clients could receive both creative and business support from one dependable team, and where people were valued for more than just the tasks they could complete.

That vision became VYRA Collective.

VYRA represents Victory + Rise and carries the belief of being “Victorious, Yet Rising Always.” For Katherine, victory does not mean reaching a point where there is nothing left to learn. It means continuing to grow, remaining humble, helping others rise, and pursuing excellence with purpose.

Her leadership is also deeply rooted in faith. She believes that VYRA is not simply a collection of people who happened to meet, but a team brought together for a purpose. This is why the culture she is building centers on God at the center, people at the heart, excellence in the work, and victory together.

Today, Katherine leads VYRA Collective with the perspective of someone who has experienced the journey from employee, to remote professional, to team leader, and ultimately to founder.

She understands what it means to serve a client because she has spent years doing it herself. She understands what it means to build from the ground up because VYRA began with experience, faith, persistence, and a vision—not a large corporation behind it.

And as VYRA continues to grow, her mission remains clear:

To build more than a successful agency—to build a team where people can discover their potential, businesses can find support they can truly rely on, and everyone has the opportunity to rise together.

Rise with purpose. Win together.

Allyza P. Cortez

Social Media Manager, VYRA Collective

Allyza P. Cortez is a Social Media Manager at VYRA Collective whose story is a reminder that you do not have to know everything before you begin you simply need the courage and willingness to learn.

Allyza only began her Virtual Assistant journey this year.

When she first started, the digital world was completely new to her. Even some of the simplest computer functions things many people might take for granted, including using the right-click button on a mouse were unfamiliar.

But what stood out was never what Allyza didn't know.

It was how willing she was to learn.

With Katherine guiding and training her along the way, Allyza started from the basics. She listened, practiced, asked questions, made mistakes, tried again, and continued showing up.

Little by little, the things that once felt unfamiliar became part of her everyday work.

From learning basic tools and navigating different platforms, she progressed into social media management, content-related tasks, client support, and the systems used within VYRA.

Today, Katherine can confidently entrust Allyza with responsibilities and allow her to work independently on tasks. More importantly, she has grown to the point where she now helps handle VYRA's own clients—a responsibility that once may have seemed far beyond where she started.

Her journey is still beginning, and that is exactly what makes her story meaningful.

Allyza does not pretend to know everything. She approaches her work with humility, curiosity, and a genuine desire to improve. She is willing to receive correction, learn something new, and keep pursuing growth even when something feels difficult at first.

At VYRA, we believe potential is not measured only by how much someone already knows.

Sometimes, potential is found in the person who says, “I don't know how to do this yet, but I'm willing to learn.”

Allyza represents that kind of growth.

From learning the basics of using a computer to becoming someone trusted with real responsibilities and real clients, her progress proves how much can change when someone is given an opportunity, receives the right guidance, and chooses to take learning seriously.

She is still learning.
She is still developing.
She is still rising.

And there is something powerful about watching a beginner slowly become someone others can depend on.

Her story at VYRA has only just begun, but she has already shown us an important lesson:

Never underestimate where a willing heart, consistent effort, and the courage to start can take you.

You don't have to start as an expert. You just have to be willing to start—and keep rising.

Rise with purpose. Win together.

Because at VYRA, we believe that you don't have to be great at everything to become valuable at something.

Everyone has their own strengths waiting to be discovered.

For Karla, those strengths became clearer through organization, following systems and procedures, administrative work, customer engagement, and making sure responsibilities are handled properly and consistently.

Today, the same person who once struggled with confidence is now communicating with customers, assisting with administrative responsibilities, following internal systems, and helping handle VYRA's clients.

She may have started quietly, but she did not stay where she started.

Karla continued learning. She continued showing up. She allowed herself to be corrected, guided, and challenged and little by little, the things that once felt difficult became responsibilities she could handle with greater independence.

Her journey is still continuing, and Katherine is still there to guide and mentor her. But Karla is no longer simply learning how to do the work.

She is becoming someone the team can trust with the work.

Her story represents something deeply important to VYRA:

Not everyone grows at the same speed. Not everyone carries the same strengths. You don't have to be the loudest, the fastest, or the person who knows everything from the beginning. Karla started as someone who needed guidance at almost every step.

Today, she is helping serve the very clients VYRA was built to support.

She didn't have to become someone else to grow. She simply had to discover what she was capable of and keep going.
