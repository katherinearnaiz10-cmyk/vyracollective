ABOUT VYRA

Victory + Rise

VYRA Collective is a creative and administrative virtual support agency built around a simple idea: people from different backgrounds rising together, using their God-given gifts, and creating victories for themselves and the businesses they serve.

Our team is made up of talented professionals from different countries, working together across borders with one shared purpose. Though we come from different places, we bring our skills, perspectives, and strengths together as one collective — supporting our clients, growing together, and creating meaningful results.

Collective reflects the people behind the work — creatives, editors, designers, virtual assistants, marketers, and support professionals working together with purpose.

God at the center. People at the heart. Excellence in the work. Victory together.

Katherine E. Arnaiz

Founder & Creative Director, VYRA Collective

Katherine E. Arnaiz is the Founder and Creative Director of VYRA Collective, bringing more than nine years of experience in customer service, business support, virtual assistance, and creative digital work.

Her professional journey began in the BPO industry, where she spent nine years working onsite and developed a strong foundation in customer service, communication, problem-solving, professionalism, and working under pressure. Those years taught her something that would later become essential to building her own company: excellent service is not simply about completing a task—it is about understanding people, taking responsibility, and delivering work with care.

Eventually, Katherine transitioned from the traditional corporate environment into the work-from-home and Virtual Assistant industry. That transition opened the door for her to work with clients and teams across different countries, including Australia, the United States, Singapore, and Korea.

Her experience expanded far beyond customer service. Over time, she worked across administrative support, social media management, content creation, graphic design, video editing, client communication, project coordination, and digital operations. Working with different businesses, cultures, industries, and expectations gave her a deeper understanding of what business owners truly need from a remote team: people who are dependable, adaptable, proactive, and genuinely invested in the work.

But VYRA Collective was not built simply because Katherine wanted to start an agency. It was built from everything she learned along the way.

After years of being the person behind the screen supporting other businesses, she began envisioning something bigger: a company where talented people could grow together, where clients could receive both creative and business support from one dependable team, and where people were valued for more than just the tasks they could complete.

That vision became VYRA Collective.

VYRA represents Victory + Rise and carries the belief of being “Victorious, Yet Rising Always.” For Katherine, victory does not mean reaching a point where there is nothing left to learn. It means continuing to grow, remaining humble, helping others rise, and pursuing excellence with purpose.

Her leadership is also deeply rooted in faith. She believes that VYRA is not simply a collection of people who happened to meet, but a team brought together for a purpose. This is why the culture she is building centers on God at the center, people at the heart, excellence in the work, and victory together.

Today, Katherine leads VYRA Collective with the perspective of someone who has experienced the journey from employee, to remote professional, to team leader, and ultimately to founder.

She understands what it means to serve a client because she has spent years doing it herself. She understands what it means to build from the ground up because VYRA began with experience, faith, persistence, and a vision—not a large corporation behind it.

And as VYRA continues to grow, her mission remains clear:

To build more than a successful agency—to build a team where people can discover their potential, businesses can find support they can truly rely on, and everyone has the opportunity to rise together.

Rise with purpose. Win together.

Dennis M. Francisco

Co-Founder & Business Development, VYRA Collective

Dennis M. Francisco is the Co-Founder and Business Development lead of VYRA Collective, an entrepreneur whose experience has been shaped by building businesses from the ground up, navigating challenges firsthand, and learning what it truly takes to turn an idea into something sustainable.

As the owner of Sushiyal and High Cultured Philippines, Dennis understands entrepreneurship beyond strategy and planning. He understands the long hours, uncertainty, discipline, decision-making, and persistence required when building something from nothing.

His journey in business taught him that growth rarely happens overnight. It is built through consistency, calculated decisions, resilience, and the willingness to keep moving even when the path ahead is not always clear.

That experience became an important part of what he brings to VYRA Collective.

As Katherine Arnaiz’s business partner, Dennis plays a key role in strengthening the business development and internal operations of VYRA. While Katherine leads the company’s creative direction, vision, and client experience, Dennis helps build the structure behind that vision—developing strategies, improving internal processes, identifying opportunities for growth, and helping the company make sound business decisions as it expands.

His role is not simply about growing the numbers.

It is about helping build a company with a strong foundation.

Dennis believes that a successful business should be able to grow without losing the values, discipline, and people that helped build it. His entrepreneurial experience allows him to look at VYRA from both perspectives: as someone building the company and as a business owner who understands what clients experience behind the scenes.

Together with Katherine and the VYRA team, he is helping transform VYRA from a vision into an organization designed to create opportunities not only for its clients, but also for the people growing within it.

For Dennis, business development is ultimately about building something that can last: creating stronger systems, developing people, opening new opportunities, and making sure every step forward has purpose behind it.

Because meaningful businesses are not simply launched. They are built decision by decision, challenge by challenge, and victory by victory.

Rise with purpose. Win together.
